# Staggered sample followers — retired 2026-10-12

Superseded by the user's requirement for independent consent and no fake friends
or messages. The automatic arrival scheduler and sample friendship action have
been removed. Existing simulated incoming relationships and their notifications
are migrated out once; user-controlled outgoing follows and user-authored messages
are retained. Legacy Codable fields remain only for reading existing journals.

See [Safety and consent](safety-and-consent.md) for the current behavior and
service/verification boundaries. Do not re-enable automatic incoming followers.
